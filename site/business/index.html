Eligibility

  • Sponsored placements do not override organic ranking logic.
  • Submitted business materials must comply with licensing, accuracy, and category policy requirements.
  • Ad, sponsored, and partnership labels are applied clearly in product surfaces.
  • Applications may be rejected after editorial, legal, or trust review.

Information We Need

  • Business name and city / district
  • Official website or social media account
  • Licensed media or promotional assets
  • Primary contact person and reply email
