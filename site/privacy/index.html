Turkce

Toplanan Veriler

  • Hesap verisi: e-posta, kullanici kimligi, oturum bilgisi.
  • Uygulama ici veri: favoriler, check-in'ler, gezi planlari, kaydedilen rotalar, yorumlar, puanlar, topluluk yazilari ve yer hikayeleri.
  • Gorsel veri: kullanici tarafindan yuklenen fotograf ve galeri dosyalari.
  • Konum verisi: yalnizca uygulama acikken yakin oneriler, harita ve rota deneyimi icin kullanilir.
  • Cihaz ve tani koyma verisi: hata kayitlari, uygulama surumu ve teknik teşhis bilgileri.
  • Bildirim verisi: push token ve kullanicinin izin tercihleri.

Kullanim Amaçlari

  • Kişiselleştirilmis gezi oneri ve planlari uretmek.
  • Harita, trend harita, kaydedilen yerler ve rota ekranlarini calistirmak.
  • Topluluk yazilarini, fotoğraflari ve yer hikayelerini moderasyon sonrasi yayina almak.
  • Suistimal, spam, telif ihlali ve guvenlik risklerini azaltmak.
  • Urun performansini ve hata teshisini iyilestirmek.

Turkce Devam

Ucuncu Taraf Hizmetler

  • Supabase: kimlik dogrulama, veritabani, storage ve backend islevleri.
  • OpenStreetMap tabanli harita verisi.
  • OSRM veya esdeger rota hesaplama servisi.
  • Firebase / push altyapisi aktifse bildirim teslimi icin ilgili servisler.
  • Reklam veya sponsorlu yuzeyler aktifse ilgili reklam teknolojileri, yalnizca aciklanan kapsam dahilinde kullanilir.
  • Bu servislerin user data'ya eristigi olculerde, kendi rolleri ile sinirli isleme yapmalari beklenir ve Routevia tarafindan makul gizlilik/guvenlik korumalari uygulanir.

Kullanici Haklari ve Saklama

  • Kullanici uygulama icinden hesap silme talebi olusturabilir.
  • Topluluk icerikleri moderasyon, hukuki yukumluluk veya telif incelemesi sebebiyle kaldirilabilir.
  • Hesap silme sonrasi kisisel veri silinir; kisitli teknik loglar kisa sureli guvenlik amacli tutulabilir.
  • Kullanici routevia@tabserve.com.tr adresinden erisim, duzeltme veya silme taleplerini iletebilir.
  • Guvenlik, dolandiricilik onleme, faturalama veya yasal uyum sebepleriyle tutulmasi gereken sinirli kayitlar gerekli sure kadar saklanabilir.

  • Kullanici dilerse routevia@tabserve.com.tr adresinden veriye erisim, duzeltme, silme ve itiraz taleplerini iletebilir.

English

Data We Collect

  • Account data such as email address, user id, and session information.
  • In-app content such as saved places, check-ins, trip plans, ratings, reviews, community posts, and place stories.
  • Media uploaded by users, including cover photos and gallery images.
  • Location data used only while the app is active to power nearby discovery, maps, and routing features.
  • Technical diagnostics such as app version, device-level error details, and security logs.
  • Push notification tokens and consent preferences where applicable.

How We Use Data

  • To deliver personalized travel discovery, route planning, and map features.
  • To operate moderated community features including stories, guides, photos, and place notes.
  • To generate Routevia trend scores from in-app community signals such as check-ins, reviews, and photos.
  • To detect abuse, spam, copyright issues, and security threats.
  • To improve reliability, customer support, and product quality.

Third Parties

  • Supabase for authentication, database, storage, and backend services.
  • OpenStreetMap-based mapping data and related routing infrastructure.
  • Push delivery services when notifications are enabled.
  • Advertising or sponsorship services only when clearly disclosed in product surfaces.
  • Where third parties process user data for Routevia features, they are expected to apply protections consistent with their role and the safeguards described in this policy.

Your Rights

  • You can delete your account inside the app or by contacting routevia@tabserve.com.tr.
  • You can request access, correction, or deletion of personal data.
  • Approved community content may be removed for moderation, legal review, or copyright compliance.
  • Some limited logs may be retained for a short time for fraud prevention or legal obligations.
  • Some records may be retained for security, fraud prevention, billing, or legal compliance where required.

English Additional Details

Sharing and Security

  • We do not sell personal data.
  • We aim to protect data using reasonable technical and organizational safeguards such as access control, session-based authentication, and protected backend infrastructure.
  • Where analytics, ads, push, payments, or support tools are used, data sharing is intended to be limited to what is needed for the relevant feature.

Retention and Deletion

  • Account data and user content may be retained while the account is active to provide the service.
  • After an account deletion request, personal data associated with the account is deleted or irreversibly anonymized, except where retention is required by law, fraud prevention, security, or billing obligations.
  • Users may contact routevia@tabserve.com.tr to request access, correction, deletion, or objection regarding their personal data.

Trend and Community Signal Disclosure

  • Bu skorlar gercek zamanli fiziksel insan yogunlugu veya resmi footfall olcumu degildir.
